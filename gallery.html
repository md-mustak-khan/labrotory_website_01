21 Curated Captures · 2022 – 2026 · University of Chittagong & BioPC

This visual collection documents my multidisciplinary research journey, spanning hands-on wet-lab benchwork at the Laboratory of Food Bioactives and Molecular Health (cu.ac.bd/fbmh), to scientific dissemination at conferences, workshops, and my MS thesis defense.

From maintaining synchronized C. elegans cultures and recording fluorescence microscopy images to mentoring students through BioPC training programs, these moments reflect the dedication, collaboration, and scientific curiosity that drive our academic discoveries.
