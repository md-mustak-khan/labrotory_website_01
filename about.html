Researcher Profile

Md. Mustak Khan

Research Assistant & Lab Manager · University of Chittagong
Founding Member & Instructor · BioPC Bioinformatics Lab

I am an early-career researcher in Biochemistry and Molecular Biology at the University of Chittagong, focused on bridging experimental wet-lab rigor with translational computational biology. My research philosophy is rooted in exploring nature-derived bioactive compounds and decoding their therapeutic mechanisms against oxidative stress, aging, and metabolic disorders.

In the wet lab, I specialize in Caenorhabditis elegans culture, longevity profiling, stress resistance assays, and spectrophotometric antioxidant evaluations. In computational biology, I drive computer-aided drug design (CADD), molecular docking simulations, ADMET pharmacokinetics, and network pharmacology pipelines to uncover disease targets and optimize lead candidates.
