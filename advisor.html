Academic Mentorship

Principal Investigators & Research Advisors

My scientific development, experimental wet-lab rigor, and translational computational mindset have been profoundly shaped by the mentorship of two esteemed principal investigators at the Department of Biochemistry and Molecular Biology, University of Chittagong. Their joint supervision guided my MS thesis research and established the foundation of my career in biomedical science.

Dr. Dwaipayan Sikdar

Professor · Department of Biochemistry and Molecular Biology, University of Chittagong

In Vitro Biochemistry Phytochemical Profiling Enzyme Kinetics & Assays Antioxidant Radical Scavenging Natural Products

Dr. Dwaipayan Sikdar is a distinguished Professor whose research program centers on natural product chemistry, in vitro enzyme inhibition assays, and antioxidant biochemistry. As my primary MS thesis supervisor, he provided rigorous academic leadership, directed the structural and scientific synthesis of my manuscripts, and provided continuous intellectual mentorship that defined my analytical perspective.

MS Thesis Supervised

"In silico and in vivo study of antioxidant activity of phytochemicals from Houttuynia cordata, a perennial herb using molecular docking and Caenorhabditis elegans model"

Email Google Scholar ResearchGate ORCID

Dr. Suman Mojumder

Associate Professor · Dept. of Biochemistry & Molecular Biology & PI, Laboratory of Applied Biosciences

C. elegans Model Biology In Vivo Longevity Assays 37 °C Survival Kinetics Thrashing Locomotion Brood Size Toxicity Applied Biosciences Lab

Dr. Suman Mojumder heads the Laboratory of Applied Biosciences at the University of Chittagong, spearheading investigations into Caenorhabditis elegans genetic models, oxidative stress defense, and lifespan regulation. As my MS thesis supervisor, he provided hands-on guidance with C. elegans culturing, thermal survival, thrashing locomotion, and reproductive toxicity assays, playing an indispensable role in my laboratory training.

MS Thesis Supervised · In Vivo Direction

"In silico and in vivo study of antioxidant activity of phytochemicals from Houttuynia cordata, a perennial herb using molecular docking and Caenorhabditis elegans model"
